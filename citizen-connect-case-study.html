CIVIC PRODUCT DESIGN 5 MIN READ

Citizen Connect

Empowering citizens to engage with local government through transparent communication, issue reporting, project tracking, and community participation.

ROLE UX/UI Designer & UX Researcher
DURATION 8 Weeks
PLATFORM Mobile Application (iOS & Android)
TOOLS Figma, FigJam, Photoshop

"Building transparent communication channels between local government units and active citizens."

01 / BACKGROUND & PROBLEM

Bridging the Civic Gap

Citizen Connect is a modern civic engagement platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and local government. In most municipalities, public information remains fragmented across outdated websites, social media pages, and physical bulletin boards.

The Real-World Problems

  • Scattered Government Information: Citizens struggle to find verified local announcements and project statuses.
  • Slow Community Reporting: Reporting potholes, broken streetlights, or waste management issues requires in-person visits or phone queues.
  • Opaque Project Progress: Infrastructure budgets and timelines are rarely accessible to the public.
  • Low Public Participation: Citizens feel disconnected from local decision-making and community polls.

Design Challenge: How might we create a mobile platform that encourages citizens to actively participate in local governance while making public information transparent and accessible?

02 / OBJECTIVES & ROLE

Measurable Goals & Responsibilities

1-Tap Community Reporting
100% Transparent Tracker
Real-time Local Announcements

Core UX Responsibilities

User Research & Competitive Analysis

Conducted 12 surveys and 5 in-depth user interviews to map civic pain points and benchmarked 4 existing portals.

Information Architecture & Flows

Structured the app sitemap, user journey maps, and streamlined issue reporting flows.

Visual Design System & Testing

Built an accessible 8pt grid design system in Figma and conducted usability testing with 6 diverse citizens.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

Double Diamond Methodology

1. DISCOVER

User Research & Surveys

12 survey respondents & 5 interviews revealing citizens rely primarily on unverified Facebook pages for city news.

2. DEFINE

Personas & Journey Mapping

Mapped friction points during issue reporting and converted insights into key HMW opportunities.

3. DEVELOP

Wireframing & Design System

Created low-fidelity sketches, sitemaps, and an accessible civic color palette (Government Blue `#1e40af`, Emerald Green `#10b981`).

4. DELIVER

Hi-Fi UI & Usability Testing

Tested interactive prototypes with 6 users to validate report submission speed and notification clarity.

04 / STRATEGY & COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

Benchmarking Existing Portals

Platform Type Strengths Shortcomings Citizen Connect Opportunity
Municipal Web Portals Official source of documentation Not mobile responsive, complex navigation Mobile-first 1-tap navigation
Social Media Pages Fast updates & high reach Unorganized, unverified comments & noise Verified broadcast notification channels
Phone Helpline Services Direct human response Long wait queues, zero status tracking Live visual report status tracker
05 / VISUAL DESIGN & INTERACTION

High-Fidelity Interface Showcase

Civic Home Dashboard

Immediate access to urgent city broadcasts, active community polls, and 1-tap issue reporting.

Photo & GPS Issue Reporter

Snap a photo of infrastructure damage, auto-detect location via GPS, and track repair status in real time.

06 / IMPACT & LEARNINGS

Projected Impact & Key Reflections

Design Targets & Expected Outcomes

  • 60% Faster Reporting: Simplified photo + GPS pinning reduces report submission time to under 45 seconds.
  • Increased Public Transparency: Real-time progress trackers build public trust in municipal infrastructure projects.
  • Inclusive Accessibility: 8pt grid typography and contrast-checked components accommodate users across all digital literacy levels.

Final Reflection

Designing Citizen Connect reinforced that transparency builds trust. Designing for public services requires balancing complex data with ultra-simple navigation, proving that UX thinking can transform how communities interact with local government.
