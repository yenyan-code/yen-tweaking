EDTECH PLATFORM 4 MIN READ

Eskwela Kit

Streamlining academic resources, schedule tracking, and student task management for Filipino learners.

ROLE Product Designer & Lead UI
DURATION 4 Weeks
PLATFORM Web & Mobile Application
TOOLS Figma, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
01 / OVERVIEW

Empowering Student Productivity

Eskwela Kit was designed to solve the chaos of managing academic workloads across fragmented messaging groups and portal announcements. It provides students with a central hub for class schedules, project deadlines, lecture notes, and study group collaboration.

"A unified digital toolkit designed for high school and university students to keep track of assignments without missing a deadline."

02 / CORE FEATURES

Designed for Academic Success

Smart Class Schedule & Reminders

Automated schedule sync with push notifications before lectures, exam dates, and assignment deadlines.

Collaborative Study Hub

Share lecture notes, organize group project tasks, and pin study guides in a shared workspace.

Digital Resource Library & Grade Tracker

Track subject grades in real-time with automated GPA projection and quick-access PDF lecture repositories.

03 / RESULTS

Impact & Student Feedback

92% On-Time Assignment Submissions
4.8★ Student Satisfaction Rating
15k+ Active Student Users
