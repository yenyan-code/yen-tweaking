CASE STUDY 4 MIN READ

Booth Platform

Hassle-Free Event Hosting & Member Check-in for College Clubs

ROLE Product Designer & Lead Frontend
TIMELINE 4-Week Design & Build Sprint
PLATFORM Web Dashboard & Mobile App
TOOLS Figma, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
01 / BACKGROUND

The Challenge

Organizing campus club events used to mean juggling disjointed tools — Google Forms for RSVPs, Instagram DMs for questions, and manual paper check-in sheets at the door. Club leaders suffered from high drop-off rates and lost track of member engagement metrics.

Booth was built to unify the entire event lifecycle into a single, intuitive experience: fast event publishing, digital QR pass tickets, and 1-tap door scanning for officers.

"We wanted to eliminate the 15-minute door bottleneck at club meetings and turn check-ins into an instant scan."

02 / CORE FEATURES

Streamlined for Officers & Students

Instant Event Builder

Publish club workshops, social mixers, and general meetings in under 60 seconds with customizable capacity limits and tags.

Digital QR Pass & 1-Tap Check-in

Attendees receive a native mobile QR pass added to their wallet, enabling event hosts to scan arrivals instantly without delays.

Live Attendance & Retention Analytics

Officers gain real-time visibility into peak arrival times, popular event formats, and returning student engagement levels.

03 / IMPACT

Results & Takeaways

85% Faster Door Check-in
12+ Campus Clubs Onboarded
4.9★ Student Satisfaction Rating

By focusing on speed, clear visual feedback, and zero-friction onboarding, Booth transformed how student organizations interact with their communities.
